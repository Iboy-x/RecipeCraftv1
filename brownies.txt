Fudgy Cocoa Brownies Recipe
serving size: 1 
Ingredients:
sugar 200 g
butter 115 g
cocoa powder 60 g
egg 2 pieces
vanilla extract 5 ml
all-purpose flour 60 g
salt 2 g
----------
Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 175°C (350°F) and grease an 8x8 inch baking pan or line it with parchment paper.
2. Melt the butter in a microwave or saucepan, then transfer it to a mixing bowl.
3. Add the sugar and cocoa powder to the melted butter and whisk thoroughly until well combined.
4. Add the eggs one at a time, whisking vigorously after each addition until the batter becomes smooth and slightly shiny. Stir in the vanilla extract.
5. Gently fold in the all-purpose flour and salt using a spatula just until the flour disappears. Do not overmix.
6. Pour the batter into your prepared baking pan and smooth the top.
7. Bake for 20 to 22 minutes. A toothpick inserted into the center should come out with a few moist crumbs, but no wet batter.
8. Allow the brownies to cool completely in the pan before slicing into squares and serving.
