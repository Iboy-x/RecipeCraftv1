Gulab Jamun Recipe
serving size: 1 
Ingredients:
milk powder 50 g
all-purpose flour 10 g
ghee 5 g
milk 20 ml
baking powder 1 g
sugar 100 g
water 100 ml
cardamom powder 1 g
oil 150 ml
----------
Instructions:
1. Mix sugar, water, and cardamom powder in a saucepan. Bring to a boil and simmer for 5 minutes to make a sticky syrup. Keep warm.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine milk powder, all-purpose flour, and baking powder.
3. Rub the ghee into the dry mixture using your fingertips until crumbly.
4. Add milk gradually and combine gently to form a soft, smooth dough. Do not over-knead.
5. Divide the dough and roll into small, smooth balls without any cracks.
6. Heat oil in a deep pan on very low heat. 
7. Fry the dough balls, stirring continuously, until they are evenly golden brown.
8. Remove the balls from the oil and immediately submerge them into the warm sugar syrup. Let soak for 1 hour before serving.
