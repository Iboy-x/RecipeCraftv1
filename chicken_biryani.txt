Chicken Biryani Recipe
serving size: 1 
Ingredients:
basmati rice 100 g
chicken (bone-in) 150 g
onion 1 piece
tomato 1 piece
yogurt 40 g
ginger-garlic paste 10 g
biryani masala powder 15 g
oil 20 ml
water 200 ml
salt 4 g
----------
Instructions:
1. Wash and soak the basmati rice in water for 30 minutes, then drain.
2. Heat oil in a pot and fry sliced onions until golden brown. Remove half for garnishing.
3. Add ginger-garlic paste and chicken to the remaining onions, sautéing until the chicken changes color.
4. Stir in chopped tomatoes, yogurt, biryani masala powder, and salt. Cook until chicken is tender and oil separates.
5. In a separate pot, boil water with a pinch of salt, add rice, and cook until 80% done, then drain.
6. Layer the cooked rice over the chicken masala base in the pot. Top with reserved fried onions.
7. Cover tightly with a lid and cook on very low heat (dum) for 12 to 15 minutes.
8. Gently fluff the biryani and serve hot.
