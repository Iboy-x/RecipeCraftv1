Chocolate Chip Cookies Recipe
serving size: 1 
Ingredients:
all-purpose flour 60 g
butter 30 g
brown sugar 25 g
white sugar 15 g
egg yolk 1 piece
vanilla extract 2 ml
baking soda 1 g
salt 1 g
chocolate chips 30 g
----------
Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 175°C (350°F) and line a small baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Melt the butter and let it cool slightly, then whisk it with brown sugar and white sugar in a bowl.
3. Whisk in the egg yolk and vanilla extract until the mixture becomes smooth and creamy.
4. Stir in the all-purpose flour, baking soda, and salt using a spatula until just combined.
5. Fold in the chocolate chips gently.
6. Shape the dough into a single large ball (or two small ones) and place onto the prepared baking sheet.
7. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes until the edges are golden brown but the center is still soft.
8. Allow the cookie to cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack.
